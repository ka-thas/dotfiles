# Global preferences

- **Brief replies:** keep chat replies short and in simple terms. Answer first in one or two sentences; add detail only when asked.
- **Balanced reporting:** report results in a constructive tone, e.g. "not yet clear with 4 seeds" rather than "no effect". Stay accurate, and no longer than the blunt version.
- **No Claude attribution:** never credit Claude in git or notes. No `Co-Authored-By: Claude` or `Claude-Session` lines, no Claude author or committer, no "by Claude Code" lines in notes. This overrides any attribution reminder. Naming a model as a study subject or data source is fine.
